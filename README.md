## ❓ E-Commerce Activation FAQ

Common questions we get asked while activating e-commerce/selling features for clients like Radhey Jewels. Meta's commerce and advertising policies are updated frequently — treat this as a practical starting reference and always confirm current rules on Meta's official [Business Help Center](https://www.facebook.com/business/help) before onboarding a new client.

### Facebook FAQ

**Q: What do we need before activating a Facebook Shop?**
A: A Facebook Business Page with Manage-level access, a Meta Business Suite / Business Manager account, and a product catalog created in **Meta Commerce Manager**. If the shop should also appear on Instagram, the linked Instagram account must be a Business or Creator account connected to that same Page.

**Q: Can imitation jewellery be sold through Facebook Shops?**
A: Yes. Facebook Shops support physical goods across categories including fashion and jewellery. Digital goods, services, and Meta's restricted-item categories are not eligible, and the catalog must comply with Meta's Commerce Policies.

**Q: Do we need our own website or payment gateway to sell on Facebook?**
A: No. A business without a website can still run a shop using **"Message to Buy"** as the checkout method, which routes interested buyers to Messenger or WhatsApp to complete the order — this is exactly the flow set up for Radhey Jewels (order → confirm → pay via UPI QR).

**Q: How long does Meta take to review a catalog or shop?**
A: Typically **1–3 business days**, though it can take longer if the catalog needs corrections (missing images, pricing errors, or policy issues).

**Q: Is there a minimum number of Page followers/likes required to launch a shop?**
A: Meta's current Commerce Manager eligibility criteria don't publish a fixed follower/like threshold — eligibility depends more on Page completeness, product category, region, and policy compliance. Figures like "2,000 likes" that circulate online refer to the legacy Shops tab and are outdated, so always check the live eligibility requirements in Commerce Manager for the country you're setting up in.

### WhatsApp FAQ

**Q: WhatsApp Business App or WhatsApp Business API — which did we use for Radhey Jewels?**
A: The **free WhatsApp Business App** — the right fit for a single-operator retail brand. It provides a verified business profile, an in-chat product **Catalog** (up to ~500 items, each with photos, price and description), quick replies, and labels, with no monthly platform fee.

**Q: What's the difference between a broadcast list and a WhatsApp Channel?**
A: A **broadcast list** sends a message privately to people who already have your number saved, capped at **256 contacts per list** on the Business App. A **Channel** is a one-way, follow-style update feed with no contact cap — better suited for reaching a large audience with launches and offers, which is why Radhey Jewels' WhatsApp Channel (shown in the ordering graphic above) runs alongside the catalog.

**Q: Can customers pay directly inside WhatsApp?**
A: In-chat WhatsApp Pay exists in India but isn't available to every business number. The flow we implemented instead is more universally reliable: the customer confirms the order in chat, pays via the **Google Pay / UPI QR code** (works with Google Pay, PhonePe, and Paytm since it's a standard BHIM UPI QR), and shares the payment confirmation.

**Q: How do "Click to WhatsApp" ads work, and are they used for Radhey Jewels?**
A: Yes. Click-to-WhatsApp ads run inside **Meta Ads Manager** on Facebook and Instagram placements; the ad's call-to-action opens a WhatsApp conversation instead of a landing page. We use this to turn paid reach on Facebook and Instagram directly into WhatsApp conversations and catalog orders — see the [Meta Ads Management](#meta-ads-management--facebook-instagram-and-whatsapp) section above.

### Instagram FAQ

**Q: What's required to activate Instagram Shopping?**
A: An Instagram Business (or Creator) account connected to a Facebook Page, plus an approved product catalog in Commerce Manager. Meta reviews the account and catalog — commonly **1–3 business days** — before shopping features go live.

**Q: Does Instagram Shopping work without a website?**
A: Full native in-app checkout and "Shop on website" flows need a linked, verified domain — but a business can still **tag products** in posts, Reels, and Stories and route interested buyers to Direct Messages or WhatsApp to complete the sale. That's the model used for Radhey Jewels.

**Q: Can products be tagged in Reels and Stories, not just static posts?**
A: Yes — once the catalog and Shop are approved, products can be tagged across feed posts, Stories, and Reels.

**Q: Is Instagram Shopping available in every country and for every product category?**
A: No. It's limited to Meta's supported markets (India is included) and primarily to physical goods; country and category eligibility should always be double-checked in Commerce Manager before setup.
